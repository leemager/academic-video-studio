description: Produce reusable teaching, research and information videos from an academic's sources and direction in this studio. Use for outlines, approved scripts, visuals, edits, captions and exports.
---

The academic is the director; handle the technical production. Follow the repository AGENTS.md. Use [setup](../../../docs/SETUP.md) on a new computer and [production commands](../../../docs/PRODUCTION.md) for the local pipeline.

For an outline request, first read the supplied materials and return a reviewable outline. Distinguish quotations, findings, interpretations and uncertainty. Map substantive claims to filenames and pages/sections. Identify facts likely to age; don't invent references or evidence. For reusable videos, aim for a focused explanation appropriate to the audience, with a concrete example or useful visual argument. Approximate duration is a brief, not a reason to cut necessary qualifications.

After outline review, prepare a conversational script. Obtain script approval before a long generation, unless exact text is already approved or the user authorises proceeding. Generate approved narration with [academic-voiceover](../academic-voiceover/SKILL.md), then use actual duration for scene timing. A measured benchmark is better than a generic words-per-minute guess. Preserve source text, spoken pronunciation hints and display text separately.

Use the included [Remotion skill](../remotion-studio/SKILL.md). Choose a coherent visual language for the subject: meaningful photographs, diagrams, data and readable typography. The neutral example is a technical starting point, not a required look. Avoid repeated badges, corner labels, redundant taglines and decorative micro-text. Use occasional purposeful transitions and quiet effects. Music is off by default; follow the brief. Keep the narration continuous across visual cuts.

Record asset provenance, licences and attribution. Represent archival material, real interfaces and scientific evidence accurately. Generated imagery can illustrate an idea but must not masquerade as evidence. For current or high-stakes claims, verify against authoritative sources and flag unresolved uncertainty to the academic.

Before handoff, inspect actual representative and transition frames; check text fit, contrast and timing. Read transcript discrepancies, verify selected-waveform hashes and caption spelling, and inspect the export's audio/video streams and duration. Mechanical QA cannot certify subtle accent or delivery. Present a short targeted listening review when it matters. Deliver the requested MP4, matching captions, source list and editable project. Publication is a separate authorised action.
